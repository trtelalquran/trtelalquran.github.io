Sura Ghafir

Sura Ghafir ita ce ta 40 a Mushafi. Tana da ayoyi 85 kuma tana farawa a shafi na 467. Karatuttukanta a nan: 61. Danna ▶ don saurara, ko ka sauke fayil ɗin.
