Sura Al'sajadah

Sura Al'sajadah ita ce ta 32 a Mushafi. Tana da ayoyi 30 kuma tana farawa a shafi na 415. Karatuttukanta a nan: 61. Danna ▶ don saurara, ko ka sauke fayil ɗin.
