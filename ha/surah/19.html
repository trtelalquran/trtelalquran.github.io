Sura Maryam

Sura Maryam ita ce ta 19 a Mushafi. Tana da ayoyi 98 kuma tana farawa a shafi na 305. Karatuttukanta a nan: 66. Danna ▶ don saurara, ko ka sauke fayil ɗin.
